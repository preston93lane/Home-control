How to play

Lowest score wins. The game ends when anyone reaches the target score (50, 100 or 150).

Each hand

Pick 3 cards to pass. With 4 players: left, right, across, no pass. With 3: left, right, no pass. With 5: left, right, 2 left, 2 right, no pass.

Whoever has the 2♣ leads it. Follow suit if you can; if you can't, play anything. The highest card of the suit led takes the trick and leads next.

No hearts or Q♠ on the first trick (unless you have nothing else). You can't lead hearts until a heart has been played.

Points

Each ♥ = 1 point. Q♠ = 13 points.

Shoot the moon: take every heart and the Q♠ and everyone else gets 26.

Jack of ♦ (optional): whoever takes it gets −10.

3 and 5 players

3 players: the 2♦ is removed (17 cards each). 5 players: the 2♦ and 2♠ are removed (10 cards each).

Playing a card

Tap a card to pick it up, tap it again to play it.
